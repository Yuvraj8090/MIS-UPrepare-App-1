About the Club

Built around safety, discipline, and shared adventure.

Tauch Club Arni is designed for divers who value structure without losing the joy of exploration. We create a setting where thoughtful training and club spirit naturally belong together.

Our Story

From local practice to trusted club identity

The club grew from a shared appreciation for precise dive preparation, calm team culture, and the unique conditions of Swiss alpine waters. That foundation still shapes every course, outing, and trip we organize today.

Members join for different reasons: first certifications, continued education, international travel, or a better local diving community. The club is structured to support all of those paths with consistency.

Club Snapshot

What defines Tauch Club Arni

  • Member-focused coaching with clear standards and approachable communication
  • Season planning shaped around alpine lakes, pool practice, and travel readiness
  • Safety culture based on briefing quality, buddy awareness, and calm execution
How We Work

A club experience that feels prepared at every step.

Professional preparation

Clear session plans, reliable communication, and realistic briefings keep members confident before they ever enter the water.

Respectful community

We aim for a club atmosphere that welcomes beginners, supports improvement, and respects the experience of long-time divers.

Progress with purpose

Courses and activities are designed to build ability over time, not simply add checkboxes or create pressure.

Milestones

How the year typically unfolds

Spring Pool refreshers, orientation evenings, and equipment readiness checks.
Summer Core open-water training, guided lake dives, and weekend club excursions.
Autumn and Winter Specialty training, travel planning, advanced workshops, and community events.
Club Philosophy

“Calm divers make better decisions.”

That mindset influences our briefings, our pacing, and our approach to member development. Professional polish is not just visual. It should also be felt in how information is delivered and how people are welcomed.
