Services and Programs

Training, travel, and member support with a consistent standard.

Our offer combines foundational diver education with club activities and guided expeditions. Every program is presented clearly so visitors understand what they can expect.

Course 01

Open Water Foundation

A structured entry path for new divers with pool sessions, academic support, and alpine open-water preparation.

  • Pool and theory modules
  • Cold-water readiness basics
  • Guided transition into lake diving
Course 02

Advanced and Specialty Paths

Programs for navigation, deep diving, dry suit technique, and continued skill development in more demanding conditions.

  • Navigation and buoyancy refinement
  • Deep and low-visibility procedures
  • Specialty coaching options
Course 03

Club Expeditions

Carefully curated day trips, training weekends, and destination travel for members who want to dive beyond their local routine.

  • Swiss lake weekends
  • International group travel planning
  • Briefed, supported dive schedules
